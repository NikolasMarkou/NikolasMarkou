# Electi Consulting — Content

Content creation and tracking repository for Electi Consulting's social media presence (primarily LinkedIn).

## About Electi Consulting

Electi Consulting is a technology consultancy specializing in **AI, blockchain, cryptography, and data science**. Founded in 2017 and headquartered in Limassol, Cyprus, with a UK presence in London, the firm combines academic rigor with enterprise-grade delivery.

**Mission:** Leverage AI, Blockchain, and Cryptography to help clients reach their potential.

**Website:** [electiconsulting.com](https://electiconsulting.com)

- **Theo Mourouzis, PhD** — Partner & Chief Executive Officer. Cryptographer-CEO. Themes: cryptography for operators, blockchain/DLT infrastructure, EU digital policy, executive education, personal reflection.

Voice, themes, cadence, and format are **author-specific**. See `docs/content_strategy_<author>.md` and `docs/profiles/<author>.md` for each author's playbook.
